2.4 Use case "Student orders a meal"

Actor: Student.
Precondition: the student is signed in, ordering is open (08:00-11:00), and at least one
dish has portions available.

Main flow:
1. The system shows today's menu, prices, available portions and allergens.    (FR-01, FR-07, FR-23)
2. The student selects one or more available dishes.
3. The system shows the order summary and the total amount.
4. The student confirms and chooses application payment.
5. The payment service confirms the payment.                                   (FR-02)
6. The system assigns a daily-unique order number.                             (FR-10)
7. The order appears on the kitchen screen within 5 seconds.                   (corrected FR-11)
8. The system shows the order number and confirmation to the student.

Alternative flow - payment fails: at step 5, if payment is rejected or times out, the
system creates no confirmed kitchen order, shows the failure reason and lets the student
retry once or cancel.

Postcondition: a paid order with a unique number is stored and visible to the kitchen, or
- if payment failed - no confirmed order exists and no meal is cooked.

Traceability (start of the matrix): this use case covers FR-01, FR-02, FR-04, FR-07,
FR-09, FR-10, corrected FR-11, FR-21, FR-23 and NFR-01.
